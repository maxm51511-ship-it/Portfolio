About me

I like machines I can hold, test and break on purpose. My work sits where mechanical design meets electronics and control: an assembly in Fusion 360, a motor controller on the bench, and a printed bracket that has to survive a day of testing.

I study Mechanical Engineering Technology at NJIT. In 2026 I interned at Human Friendly Robotics, a pre-seed robotics startup in Marlboro, NJ, where I led three interns building Trey and built the chassis for Tyler.

On campus I am Secretary of the NJIT Prosthetics Club and lead our animal prosthetics project. I am also a member of ASME and IEEE, and I take part in hackathons.
