About

I like machines I can hold, test and break on purpose. My work sits where mechanical design meets electronics and control: an assembly in Fusion 360, a motor controller on the bench, and a printed bracket that has to survive a day of testing.

This year I interned at Human Friendly Robotics, a pre-seed robotics startup in Marlboro, NJ. I led three interns building Trey, an autonomous data center cable-management robot, and built the chassis for Tyler, a 50 lb autonomous flooring robot.

On campus I serve as Secretary of the NJIT Prosthetics Club and lead our animal prosthetics project, with 50+ active members across the club.

What I built

  1. Nov 2026

    Autonomous 3D print removal arm

    UFactory xArm 6 with a RealSense depth camera, integrated through Viam. Trained a YOLO model on a custom dataset to tell failed prints from good ones, with a closed loop that verifies a cleared bed and restarts jobs.

  2. Sep 2026

    Groundwork 2nd of 40 teams

    Dell x NVIDIA AI Hackathon. A predictive maintenance prototype that flags bearing inner-race faults and other mechanical fault signatures from vibration data, built with a 4-person team across ECE, CS and CE.

  3. Mar to Sep 2026

    Trey, data center cable-management robot

    Led 3 interns through design, build and testing. Its Ethernet test switch verifies up to 8 cables at once. Fusion 360 assemblies with linear actuators, hinge clamps and custom brackets; RoboClaw, Teensy 4.1 and Raspberry Pi for motor control and odometry.

  4. 2026

    Tyler, autonomous flooring robot

    Assembled and wired the chassis of a 50 lb Jetson-powered robot rated for ±1 mm tile placement. Calibrated placement and added an IMU to stabilize tile pickup.

  5. Feb 2026 to present

    Custom prosthetic for a three-legged dog

    Co-lead for the NJIT Prosthetics Club. Standardized member CAD parts and tuned print settings (45% infill, 5 wall loops) for strength.

  6. Feb to Apr 2026

    NeuroMaker and BCI prosthetic hand

    Multi-articulated hand with high-torque servos and custom linkages. C++ firmware on Arduino turns EEG attention levels into gestures, with calibrated thresholds and filters to cut false activations.
