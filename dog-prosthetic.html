Custom prosthetic for a three-legged dog

WhenFeb 2026 to present
WhereNJIT Prosthetics Club
My roleCo-lead

The NJIT Prosthetics Club is designing a custom prosthetic for a three-legged dog. I co-lead the project as Secretary and Animal Prosthetics Project Lead.

What I did

  • Co-leading the design of the prosthetic.
  • Review member CAD designs and standardize parts to move teams from prototypes to full assemblies.
  • Optimized FDM print settings (45% infill, 5 wall loops) for strength and impact resistance.
  • Reached out to 3DPets and My Pet's Brace: the club toured 3DPets for advice and is working with My Pet's Brace on a client's prosthetic.
  • Manage communication and documentation between project leads and 50+ active members.
