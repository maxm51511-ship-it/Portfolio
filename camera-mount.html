Camera mount for a grouting robot

When2026
WhereHuman Friendly Robotics
My roleCAD design

A Fusion 360 design for mounting a camera on a grouting robot.

What I did

  • Iterated from an L-bracket to a flat plate with rear U-channel bosses that straddle a VEX aluminum extrusion rail.
  • Mounts an ELP 12MP USB box-housing camera with M2.5 fasteners.
  • Modeled the M2.5 holes at 2.6 mm to account for FDM undersizing.
