Trey, data center cable-management robot Team lead

WhenMar to Sep 2026
WhereHuman Friendly Robotics
My roleTeam lead, 3 interns

Trey is a tracked robot that pulls and bundles cable in data center cable trays. I led three interns through its design, build and testing.

What I did

  • Led 3 interns through design, build and testing.
  • Its Ethernet test switch verifies up to 8 cables at once.
  • Designed Fusion 360 assemblies with linear actuators, motors, hinge clamps and custom mounting brackets.
  • Integrated motors, wheel encoders and RoboClaw controllers with a Teensy 4.1 and Raspberry Pi for motor control and odometry navigation.
  • Rebuilt the drive electronics around a RoboClaw 2x7A controlled by a Teensy 4.1 over serial.
  • Bought an 18 in wide, 118 in long cable tray section to test on a representative run.
  • Maintained a 6-printer FDM fleet and tuned print settings and material (PETG), cutting failed prints from 2 to 5 per day to 1 to 2 per day, about a 50% reduction.
